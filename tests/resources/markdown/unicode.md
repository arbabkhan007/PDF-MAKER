# Unicode & International Text

Typography must survive contact with the world's writing systems.

## European scripts

- Latin with diacritics: héllo, wörld, naïve, façade, Łódź, Žižka
- Greek: αλφάβητο, φιλοσοφία, Ω≈ç√∫, 3 ≤ π < 4
- Cyrillic: Здравствуйте, мир, съешь ещё этих мягких французских булок
- Nordic: smørrebrød,lätt, omnämnandet

## Right-to-left scripts

Arabic: اللغة العربية جميلة جداً

Hebrew: שפה יפה ועתיקה

Urdu: اردو ایک خوبصورت زبان ہے

## East Asian scripts

- Chinese (simplified): 汉字是表意文字
- Chinese (traditional): 漢字是表意文字
- Japanese: 日本語のテキストは美しいです。ひらがなとカタカナと漢字。
- Korean: 한국어 텍스트가 아름답습니다

## South and Southeast Asian scripts

Hindi: सभी लोगों को गरिमा और अधिकार प्राप्त है

Thai: ภาษาไทยเป็นภาษาที่สวยงาม

## Emoji and symbols

Rocket ship: 🚀 — document: 📄 — sparkle: ✨ — books: 📚

Family: 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 (joined with zero-width joiners)

Flags: 🇵🇰 🇫🇷 🇯🇵 🇧🇷

Hearts and hands: ❤️ 💚 👍 🙏

Chess and music: ♔♕♖ ♪♫

Arrows and math: ← → ↔ ⇒ ∀∂ ∅ ∈ ⊗ ≅ ⊕

## Mixed-direction text

A sentence mixing Latin and Arabic: the word كتاب means "book", and
سلام means "peace".

## Punctuation and dashes

Quotation marks: "double" 'single' «guillemets» „German“ „Polish”

Dashes: hyphen - en – em — minus −

Currency: €25 £10 ¥1,000 ₹500 ₨ 150

Invisible characters: zero​width​space (there are two in here), and
non-breaking spaces (U+00A0).
